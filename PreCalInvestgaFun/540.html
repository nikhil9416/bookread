To divide two complex numbers, we have to devise a way to write this as a complex number with a real part and an imaginary part. We start this process by eliminating the complex number in the denominator. To do this, we multiply the numerator and denominator by a special complex number so that the result in the denominator is a real number. The number we need to multiply by is called the complex conjugate, in which the sign of the imaginary part is changed. Here, 4+i is the complex conjugate of 4–i.
