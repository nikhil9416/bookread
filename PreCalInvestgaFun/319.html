Example 2 Cesium-137 has a half-life of about 30 years. If you begin with 200mg of cesium-137, how much will remain after 30 years? 60 years? 90 years? Since the half-life is 30 years, after 30 years, half the original amount, 100mg, will remain. After 60 years, another 30 years have passed, so during that second 30 years, another half of the substance will decay, leaving 50mg.
