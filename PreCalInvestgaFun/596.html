Section 9.1 Ellipses 585 Ellipses Not Centered at the Origin Not all ellipses are centered at the origin. The graph of such an ellipse is a shift of the graph centered at the origin, so the standard equation for one centered at (h, k) is slightly different. We can shift the graph right h units and up k units by replacing x with x – h and y with y – k, similar to what we did when we learned transformations.
