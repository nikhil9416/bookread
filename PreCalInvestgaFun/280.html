Transformations of Exponential Graphs While exponential functions can be transformed following the same rules as any function, there are a few interesting features of transformations that can be identified. The first was seen at the beginning of the section – that a horizontal reflection is equivalent to a change in the growth factor. Likewise, since a is itself a stretch factor, a vertical stretch of an exponential corresponds with a change in the initial value of the function.
