Section 1.3 Rates of Change and Behavior of Graphs 43 From the tables, we can see that the rate of change for company A is decreasing, while the rate of change for company B is increasing. When the rate of change is getting smaller, as with Company A, we say the function is concave down. When the rate of change is getting larger, as with Company B, we say the function is concave up. Concavity A function is concave up if the rate of change is increasing. A function is concave down if the rate of change is decreasing. A point where a function changes from concave up to concave down or vice versa is called an inflection point.
