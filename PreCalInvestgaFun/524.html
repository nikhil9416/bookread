514 Chapter 8 Section 8.2 Polar Coordinates The coordinate system we are most familiar with is called the Cartesian coordinate system, a rectangular plane divided into four quadrants by horizontal and vertical axes. In earlier chapters, we often found the Cartesian coordinates of a point on a circle at a given angle from the positive horizontal axis. Sometimes that angle, along with the point’s distance from the origin, provides a more useful way of describing the point’s location than conventional Cartesian coordinates.
