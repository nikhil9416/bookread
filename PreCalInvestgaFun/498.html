488 Chapter 7 Section 7.4 Modeling Changing Amplitude and Midline While sinusoidal functions can model a variety of behaviors, it is often necessary to combine sinusoidal functions with linear and exponential curves to model real applications and behaviors. We begin this section by looking at changes to the midline of a sinusoidal function. Recall that the midline describes the middle, or average value, of the sinusoidal function. Changing Midlines Example 1 A population of elk currently averages 2000 elk, and that average has been growing by 4% each year.
