445 Thinking functionally You’ve seen the term functional quite frequently throughout this book. By now, you may have some ideas about what being functional entails. Is it about lambdas and first-class functions or about restricting your right to mutate objects? What do you achieve from adopting a functional style? In this chapter, we shed light on the answers to these questions. We explain what functional programming is and introduce some of its terminology. First, we exam- ine the concepts behind functional programming—such as side effects, immutabil- ity, declarative programming, and referential transparency—and then we relate these concepts to Java 8. In chapter 19, we look more closely at functional program- ming techniques such as higher-order functions, currying, persistent data struc- tures, lazy lists, pattern matching, and combinators.
